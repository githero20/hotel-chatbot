1. What is OSCA Fest?
a. OSCA Fest is an annual event organized by Open Source Community Africa, celebrating open source culture, collaboration, and innovation across Africa.

2. Who can attend OSCA Fest?
a. Anyone interested in open source, technology, and community building is welcome to attend OSCA Fest.

3. Do I need to register before attending OSCA Fest?
a. Yes, registration is required. You can register on the official festival website at https://festival.oscafrica.org/.

4. Is OSCA Fest free to attend?
a. OSCA Fest may have free and paid ticket options. Please check the registration page for the latest details.

5. When will the current OSCA Fest be hosted?
a. The 2025 OSCA Fest will be held from August 14 to August 16, 2025.

6. Where can I find the event schedule?
a. The full schedule is available at https://festival.oscafrica.org/schedule/.

7. What types of sessions are held at OSCA Fest?
a. OSCA Fest features workshops, keynote speeches, panels, hackathons, networking opportunities, and community discussions.

8. What is the OSCA Fest Code of Conduct?
a. The code of conduct outlines the values and expectations for all participants—promoting respect, inclusivity, and a harassment-free experience. Details are at https://docs.oscafrica.org/about/community-code-of-conduct.

9. What should I do if I experience or witness inappropriate behavior?
a. Report any incidents to event organizers or designated volunteers. All reports are handled confidentially and promptly.

10. Are there online sessions or is OSCA Fest in-person only?
a. OSCA Fest may include both in-person and virtual sessions—check the latest updates on the official website.

11. How can I volunteer at OSCA Fest?
a. Volunteer opportunities are announced on the festival website and OSCA’s social media channels. Watch for updates or contact the organizers directly.

12. Is OSCA Fest suitable for beginners?
a. Yes! Sessions are designed for all skill levels—including beginners, students, and experienced contributors.

13. Can I present or host a session at OSCA Fest?
a. Yes, if you are interested in presenting, look out for calls for speakers or submit a proposal via the festival website.

14. What topics are covered at OSCA Fest?
a. Topics include open source, software development, community building, diversity, design, data science, and more.

15. Are there networking opportunities?
a. Yes, OSCA Fest offers multiple opportunities to meet, connect, and collaborate with attendees and speakers.

16. How do I stay updated on OSCA Fest announcements?
a. Follow OSCA Africa on social media, join their mailing list, and check the official website regularly.

17. Are meals or refreshments provided during the event?
a. Details about meals and refreshments will be available on the event page or communicated to registered attendees.

18. What should I bring to OSCA Fest?
a. Bring your laptop, chargers, and any materials needed for workshops. Also, bring a positive attitude and readiness to learn!

19. How do I get help during the festival?
a. Event volunteers and staff will be available to assist. Look for people with OSCA badges or visit the help desk.

20. Can I take photos or record sessions?
a. Always ask for consent before photographing or recording others. Respect privacy and follow event guidelines.

21. What accessibility options are available at OSCA Fest?
a. OSCA Fest aims to be accessible to all. If you have specific needs, contact the organizers in advance for support.

22. Is there a dress code for OSCA Fest?
a. There is no strict dress code, but participants are encouraged to dress comfortably and respectfully.

23. How can I contribute to OSCA Africa outside the festival?
a. You can join the OSCA community, contribute to projects, volunteer, or participate in online discussions year-round.

24. Where can I review the OSCA Fest community guidelines?
a. The community guidelines and code of conduct are available at https://docs.oscafrica.org/about/community-code-of-conduct.

25. What languages are spoken at OSCA Fest?
a. English is the primary language, but sessions and networking may include other African languages depending on speakers.

26. How do I access session recordings after the festival?
a. Session recordings will be shared on the OSCA Fest website or social channels, subject to speaker consent.

27. How long does OSCA Fest last?
a. OSCA Fest lasts for three days, from August 14 to August 16, 2025. Daily schedules and session times are listed at https://festival.oscafrica.org/schedule/.
