# DevFest Lagos 2025 – Frequently Asked Questions

1. **What is DevFest Lagos 2025?**  
   DevFest Lagos 2025 is the next edition of the annual developer-festival organised by GDG Lagos (Google Developer Group Lagos), bringing together developers, technologists, designers, product folks, and tech enthusiasts across Nigeria (and beyond) for hands-on workshops, talks, product showcases, hackathons, and community networking.

2. **When will DevFest Lagos 2025 take place?**  
   The event is scheduled for **November 18-22, 2025** (five full days) according to the official site.

3. **Where will the event be hosted?**  
   The official venue has not been publicly confirmed in all details for 2025; historically, the event has been held at venues such as the Landmark Centre, Lagos.

4. **Who can attend DevFest Lagos 2025?**  
   Anyone interested in technology — whether you’re a beginner, student, hobbyist, professional, or industry expert — is welcome. The event spans beginner-friendly and advanced tracks.

5. **Do I need to register before attending?**  
   Yes. You must secure a ticket via the official registration/ticketing platform as specified on the event website.

6. **What types of ticket options are available?**  
   Two main ticket types are listed:

   - Standard Ticket: A daily pass open to everyone for access to talks, sessions, sponsor booths, networking.
   - Pro Ticket: A premium all-in day experience (Thursday) with exclusive access to product demos, masterclasses, executive roundtable, swag/merch.

7. **How much do tickets cost?**  
   For prior publications: The Standard Ticket was listed at ₦7,000 per day. The Pro Ticket at ₦70,000 for the Thursday experience.

8. **What is included when I buy a ticket?**  
   A ticket generally grants access to all talks/sessions on your chosen day(s), access to sponsor booths and the networking area. For the Pro ticket: additional perks such as product demos, masterclasses, executive roundtable, special merch.

9. **Can I purchase tickets for other people?**  
   Yes — the event site indicates you can buy tickets for others and even for more than 10 people (bulk/group purchases).

10. **What if I register and cannot attend anymore?**  
    The site lists the question under FAQs (“What if I register and can’t attend anymore?”) but the full policy (refunds, transfers) should be reviewed in the detailed terms.

11. **Is lunch, swag or refreshments included in the ticket?**  
    The FAQ mentions the question “Is lunch or swag included in my ticket?” but the answer is not clearly published in the snippet—check the ticketing page for exact inclusions.

12. **What kinds of sessions and activities will there be?**  
    Sessions include: bootcamps & codelabs, hands-on workshops, hackathons & solution challenges, panel sessions & global keynotes, masterclasses, startup & product showcases, community & talent day (networking/hiring), office hours with mentors, and an after-party celebration.

13. **Do I need to have prior experience or be a developer to benefit from the event?**  
    No prior deep experience is required; the event is designed to accommodate beginners through to advanced practitioners. Workshops and tracks span levels.

14. **Is the event purely in-person or is there a virtual/hybrid option?**  
    The primary format is in-person. If virtual or hybrid options are offered, the event website will provide those details.

15. **How can I stay updated on the latest announcements (speakers, schedule, ticket release)?**  
    Follow the official channels of GDG Lagos on social media (X/Twitter, Instagram), subscribe to mailing lists, and regularly check the official event website.

16. **Can I propose to speak or host a session at DevFest Lagos 2025?**  
    Yes — previous editions have invited speaker submissions. Watch for a “Call for Speakers” announcement on the official website or social channels.

17. **Are networking and talent/hiring opportunities available?**  
    Yes — one of the tracks described is “Community & Talent Day” for networking, connecting with hiring teams, showcasing skills.

18. **Is there an after-party or social event as part of DevFest?**  
    Yes — an “After Party Celebration” is included in the event line-up for closing off the festival.

19. **What topics will the event cover?**  
    The topics span AI, Cloud, Web, Mobile, DevOps, Machine Learning, AR/VR, Design, Product, Startups and more.1}

20. **Are there opportunities for hands-on learning (workshops/codelabs)?**  
    Yes — the festival strongly emphasises hands-on workshops and full-day bootcamps and codelabs.2}

21. **Where can I view the event schedule once it is published?**  
    The schedule will likely be published on the official site under a “Schedule” or similar section — check the event’s website later for details.

22. **Will there be swag or merchandise for attendees?**  
    Yes — the Pro ticket mentions “special swags and merch.” Standard ticket may also include some swag depending on the package.3}

23. **What is the daily start time or event hours?**  
    For past editions: e.g., 2024 edition sessions started early in the morning (around 9:00 AM).4} Final daily times for 2025 will be announced.

24. **Can I upgrade my ticket after purchase?**  
    The ticketing FAQ mentions “Upgrading Tickets” as a topic — check the platform or contact organisers for the upgrade process.5}

25. **Is the event accessible (for persons with special needs)?**  
    Accessibility is important — if you have special needs, reach out to the organisers in advance so they can assist with arrangements.

26. **What should I bring with me to DevFest Lagos 2025?**  
    At minimum: laptop (for workshops/codelabs), chargers, notebook, business cards (for networking), comfortable attire, and open mindset for learning and connecting.

27. **Is there a dress code for the event?**  
    There is no strict dress code listed — attendees are encouraged to dress comfortably and professionally casual.

28. **Can I record sessions or take photographs?**  
    Recording or photographing sessions may be subject to speaker and event policy. Always check session signage or ask organisers before recording others.

29. **Can I get help on-site during the event?**  
    Yes — event staff and volunteers will be available. Look out for organisers wearing event badges or visit the help/registration desk.

30. **What happens after the event? Will session recordings be available?**  
    Typically, post-event, session recordings may be shared (subject to speaker permission). Check the event website or social channels after the festival for links and updates.

- Stay updated for any changes to schedule or venue via official channels.
